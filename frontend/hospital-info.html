About HealthWay Hospital

Providing high-standard patient healthcare, clinical diagnostics, emergency treatment, and community welfare services with compassion and excellence.

24/7 Emergency & Critical Care Admissions

Our Emergency & Trauma Unit is open 24 hours every day. If you require urgent ambulance transfer or acute life-support care, contact our 24/7 Emergency Hotline immediately at +94 11 123 4567 (Ext 1) or use the designated ambulance entrance on HealthWay Boulevard.

Clinical Overview

HealthWay Hospital is a state-of-the-art multi-specialty tertiary care hospital. With over 250 in-patient beds, 13 specialized clinical departments, modular surgical suites, and advanced 3T MRI & CT imaging, we are committed to providing seamless medical services to patients and visitors.

Our Mission

To deliver high-quality, patient-centric healthcare with accessible hospital navigation.

Our Vision

To be the region's most trusted hospital, recognized for clinical excellence and patient hospitality.
